Community Fraud Awareness Summit

A free educational session for older adults

Older adults are increasingly targeted by phone, email, and online scams — and the tactics used by scammers are becoming more sophisticated every year. Many people feel unsure about what to do when something seems suspicious, or worry about making a mistake online.

This event is here to help. Our goal is to give you clear, practical knowledge so you can feel confident when navigating phone calls, emails, and the internet.

About the Event

A Community Event

This event is organized in partnership with a Utah Tech University and community organizations. It is open to the public and completely free of charge.

Nothing is being sold here. There are no products, memberships, or services being promoted. This is a straightforward educational session — designed to be simple, supportive, and practical for people of all backgrounds and levels with technology.

You are welcome to bring family members, friends, or anyone who helps care for you. The more people who attend, the safer our community becomes.

What You'll Learn

Practical Knowledge You Can Use Right Away

This session covers the topics that matter most:

  • Common phone, email, and online scams
  • Warning signs to watch for before you respond to a call, click a link, or share information
  • What to do when something feels suspicious
  • How to safely contact your bank, a government agency, or a trusted institution directly
  • How to report a scam to the appropriate authorities
  • Tips and guidance specifically for family members

No prior experience with technology is needed. Everything will be explained in simple, everyday language.
